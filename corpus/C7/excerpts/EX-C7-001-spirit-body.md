# Excerpt: Spirit-man distinct from body and soul

## Exact text

```
But man himself is not the body, nor the soul; he is a spirit and a part of Allah.
Creative Fate gave to man, to spirit-man, a soul that he might function on the plane of soul;
gave him a body of the flesh, that he might function on the plane of things made manifest.
```
