# Excerpt: The Self is not killed when the body is killed

## Exact text (Sanskrit)

```
न जायते म्रियते वा विपश्चिन् नायं कुतश्चिन्न बभूव कश्चित् ।
अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे ॥
```

## Translation (Max Müller / standard)

```
The wise is not born, nor does he die;
he has not come from anywhere, nor has he become anyone.
Unborn, eternal, everlasting, ancient,
he is not killed when the body is killed.
```
